Scoring Data Sources - AI Opportunity Matrix

Automation Feasibility
1. McKinsey 2025, "The Looming Advisor Shortage in US Wealth Management" - estimates broader technology/gen-AI adoption could produce 6–12% time savings, with additional gains from workflow automation. 
Link: https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/the-looming-advisor-shortage-in-us-wealth-management

2. McKinsey 2025, "Agents for growth: Turning AI promise into impact" - analysis shows that effective and scaled agent deployments could deliver productivity improvements of three to five percent annually and potentially lift growth by 10 percent or more.
Link: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/agents-for-growth-turning-ai-promise-into-impact

3. Deloitte 2026, "The agentic AI productivity wave is heading for wealth management" - explicitly recommends deploying agentic AI first in onboarding, service requests, and CRM follow-up because risk is lower and data is more available.
Link: https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/financial-services/financial-services-industry-predictions/2026/agentic-ai-wealth-management-productivity.html

Time & Volume Benchmarks

Capgemini, "Top Trends 2025: Wealth Management" (Capgemini Research Institute for Financial Services)
A 2024 Avaloq survey found that 29% of wealth executives take three months or more to onboard ultra-wealthy clients.
Link: https://www.capgemini.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Capgemini_Top-Trends-2025_Wealth-Management.pdf

Bain & Company, "Financial Services & Wealth Management Insights" - AI has helped wealth management companies achieve a 24%–35% increase in revenue per adviser.
Link: https://www.bain.com/industry-expertise/financial-services/wealth-asset-management/

Intelliflo, "2024 Advice Efficiency Survey" - On average, firms spend around 15 hours onboarding new clients, with 60% of that time not spent directly with the client. Breaking this figure down, advisers spend 3.5 hours on fact finding and research, 5.3 hours on report writing, 2.7 hours on presenting and recommendations and 3.5 hours on implementing and financial plans.
Link: https://www.intelliflo.com/insights/thought-leadership/intelliflos-2024-advice-efficiency-survey

McKinsey & Company, "From tailwinds to crosscurrents: Resilient growth in wealth management" - ~12 new household relationships per advisor/year, based on McKinsey's reported distribution of 1.5, 7.2, 12, and 18 new households across advisor performance quartiles.
Link: https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/from-tailwinds-to-crosscurrents-resilient-growth-in-wealth-management

Data Readiness:

Journal of Computer Science and Technology Studies, "Laying the Foundation: Why Data Readiness is the Cornerstone of Successful AI Initiatives" - The paper reports that a substantial share of AI initiatives fail to deliver expected value, with inadequate data quality and preparation identified as the primary contributor to those failures — citing Gartner's 2025 research that most AI projects are put at risk by a lack of AI-ready data.
Link: https://al-kindipublisher.com/index.php/jcsts/article/view/10198

World Economic Forum, "Why data readiness is a strategic imperative for businesses" - Despite widespread experimentation, fewer than one-in-five organizations recently reported high maturity in any aspect of data readiness, and in Capgemini's related research, more than half of business leaders cite data quality and availability as major challenges to accelerating AI adoption, with 72% saying they will prioritize data foundations and pipelines as their fastest-growing area of technical AI investment over the next 12 months.
Link: https://www.weforum.org/stories/artificial-intelligence/why-data-readiness-is-now-a-strategic-imperative-for-businesses/

IBM, "What is AI-ready Data?" - According to a 2024 survey from the IBM Institute for Business Value, only 29% of technology leaders strongly agree that their enterprise data meets the quality, accessibility and security standards needed to efficiently scale generative AI.
Link: https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/ai-ready-data



Process Friction / Failure Exposure

McKinsey & Company, "Winning corporate clients with great onboarding" - Know-your-customer (KYC) due diligence and account opening are significant bottlenecks. Banks report that more than 40 percent of the time a customer spends onboarding is consumed by these two processes.
Link: https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/winning-corporate-clients-with-great-onboarding

F2 Strategy, "Q1 2026 Trend Report" - F2 Strategy's 2026 operating-model research found that 72% of wealth-management firms surveyed identified onboarding as a key source of friction. The survey covered more than 30 wealth-management firms representing more than $1 trillion in assets.
Link: https://f2strategy.com/research/q1-2026-trend-report

Morningstar, "2023 Voice of the Investor Study" - Advisors recognize that educating new clients (44% of advisors), building trust (35%), and dedicating enough time for deep conversations (25%) represent significant challenges during the onboarding process. Advisors spend nearly half their time on business operations and investment research, limiting time for client-focused activities.
Link: https://www.morningstar.com/business/insights/blog/what-makes-a-good-financial-advisor

Risk Level Data

FINRA, "2026 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report" - includes a new section specifically addressing GenAI. FINRA says that its existing rules and securities laws continue to apply when firms use GenAI, and specifically discusses risks involving accuracy, hallucinations, bias, data quality, supervision, governance, testing, monitoring, and human-in-the-loop review.
Link: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/reports/2026-finra-annual-regulatory-oversight-report/gen-ai

FINRA, "Rule 2111 - Suitability" - requires a broker-dealer/associated person to have a reasonable basis to believe a recommended transaction or investment strategy is suitable based on the customer's investment profile, including financial situation, objectives, time horizon, liquidity needs, risk tolerance, and other relevant information.
Link: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2111

SEC, Staff Bulletin: "Standards of Conduct for Broker-Dealers and Investment Advisers Account Recommendations for Retail Investors" - The SEC explains that investment advisers are subject to a fiduciary duty under the Investment Advisers Act, while broker-dealers making retail recommendations are subject to Reg BI. The SEC also specifically addresses account recommendations and says firms need sufficient information about the retail investor to form a reasonable basis for the recommendation.
Link: https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-trading-markets/broker-dealers/staff-bulletin-standards-conduct-broker-dealers-investment-advisers-account-recommendations-retail

NIST, "Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)" - NIST specifically says the framework is designed to help organizations manage AI risks throughout the AI lifecycle. Steps include: govern, map, measure, and manage to evaluate AI component of proposed automation. 
Link: https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10
